## Exploratory rule and results
A fixed SPY 200-session SMA rule was tested: a signal known at close controls the next session, long SPY only when adjusted close is above the trailing SMA, otherwise cash; modeled cost is 5 bps per one-way position change, with zero cash yield. No parameter sweep was run.

Out-of-sample 2021-01-04 to 2026-10-07:
- Buy-and-hold: CAGR 15.12%, zero-rate Sharpe 0.930, max drawdown -24.50%, total return 124.43%.
- 200-day trend filter after modeled costs: CAGR 11.99%, zero-rate Sharpe 1.037, max drawdown -20.69%, total return 91.60%.
- Interpretation: lower volatility/drawdown and slightly higher zero-rate Sharpe, but materially lower CAGR and total return than buy-and-hold. This is a risk-control trade-off, not evidence of superior profitability.
