# Hypothesis 21 — Independent-Source Replication of H19

**Status:** PREREGISTERED BEFORE STRATEGY CALCULATION. Exact H19 rule; independent data source.

## Question
Does the fixed H19 sector cross-sectional momentum strategy retain its preregistered performance-screen result when prices are sourced independently from Yahoo Finance chart data rather than the project's local Parquet source?

## Frozen method
- Universe: XLE, XLF, XLI, XLK, XLP, XLU, XLV, XLY; benchmark SPY.
- Data source: Yahoo Finance chart endpoint, adjusted-close series. Download daily adjusted closes from 2019-01-01 through the latest available date at the time of execution. Record retrieval timestamp, response metadata, actual first/last dates, row counts, and hashes of downloaded raw files. If any required ticker is missing or has an incomplete period, fail closed.
- Convert adjusted closes to month-end values by taking the last observed trading-session adjusted close per calendar month. Exclude any partial latest month. Do not mix the old local provider's prices into this test.
- Exact H19 rule: at each month-end, rank the eight sector ETFs by trailing 12-month adjusted-close return; equal-weight the top three for the next month. One-month signal lag, no leverage or shorting, zero cash yield.
- OOS: 2021-01 through 2026-09, exactly the same 69 calendar months as H19. Require at least 12 months of pre-OOS data for signal formation.
- Costs: 10 bps per one-way traded notional, with turnover = half the sum of absolute changes in portfolio weights, including initial OOS entry. SPY benchmark pays 10 bps initial entry cost only. No further costs or parameter sweeps.
- Metrics: CAGR, total return, annual volatility, Sharpe with risk-free rate zero, max drawdown, monthly selections, and paired monthly excess returns.
- Replication criterion: all three original H19 checks must pass on the independent source: strategy net CAGR > SPY, net Sharpe > SPY, and max drawdown no worse than SPY. Otherwise report INDEPENDENT_REPLICATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED. Also report the numerical difference from H19 results.
- No lookahead, no changes to universe/lookback/top-N/costs, no strategy tuning after results.

## Safeguards and limitations
- Yahoo Finance is a separate data provider from the local Parquet source used for H19, but data corrections and methodology may differ; this is not an independent time-period test.
- Public chart endpoint access is used without paid data or a subscription. Preserve downloaded data and hashes for reproducibility; respect provider terms and rate limits.
- No capital, no paper/live trading. H8 remains BLOCKED.
